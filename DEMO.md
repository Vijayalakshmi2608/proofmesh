# ProofMesh three-minute demo

## Product sentence

**Proof once. Unlock work everywhere.** ProofMesh turns verified Web3 outcomes into reusable, privacy-preserving Proof-of-Work Receipts.

## Walkthrough

1. Open `/` and show the before/after workflow. Emphasize that repeated evidence sharing becomes one reusable receipt.
2. Open `/tasks/new` as Sponsor. Create or review an advanced Solana task with an active Solana Integration proof requirement, a 2 USDC Devnet reward, and explicit acceptance criteria.
3. Switch the Demo Mode role to Contributor from the topbar and open `/access`.
4. Show the eligibility explanation: the active receipt satisfies the rule, the issuer is approved, and private evidence remains hidden.
5. Click **Apply with proof**. Explain that the application sends eligibility metadata, not repository contents or screenshots.
6. Open `/submissions/sub-001` as Reviewer. Show deterministic checks, human review, and the status timeline.
7. Open `/proofs` and show the Proof Passport: active capability, revoked capability, reuse count, and recent verification history.
8. Open `/proofs/proof-001`, then **Verify proof publicly**. The public route works without authentication and shows only privacy-safe receipt metadata.
9. Open `/graph` and explain how one receipt unlocks future work and remains reusable after settlement.
10. Open `/agents`. Request a small task, then increase the reward above the approval threshold to show the explicit human approval state.
11. Close on `/settings` and point out Demo Mode reset, Devnet disclosure, and prototype limitations.

## Talking points

- Eligibility is evaluated from server-side policy, not trusted browser state.
- Active and revoked proofs produce different access outcomes.
- Sponsors see proof status and capability metadata, never private evidence.
- Financial actions are labeled Demo transaction and are not real production settlement.
- The simulated agent cannot modify outcome contracts, change recipients, bypass proof rules, or approve its own work.
